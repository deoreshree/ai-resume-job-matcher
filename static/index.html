About

Private, explainable career support

Private by design

Resumes are processed in memory and never written to disk. Nothing is stored, logged, or shared. Close the tab and the analysis is gone.

Transparent scoring

Scores combine Skills (40%), Semantic similarity (25%), Experience (15%), Education (10%), and Keywords (10%). Every result shows the breakdown.

Honest guidance

Recommendations are grounded in evidence found in your resume. The tool never suggests adding skills you cannot back up in an interview.

Works offline

Matching, ATS analysis, and interview questions need no API keys. AI-written advice is an optional enhancement.

Good to know

  • Scanned or image-only PDFs are not supported yet (no OCR).
  • Unusual layouts may reduce parsing accuracy — conventional section headings work best.
  • The skill catalog and role list are plain JSON files, easy to extend.
  • Results are advisory; always review before acting on them.
